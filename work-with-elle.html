How I Work

I take what you've built seriously.

Every business has a history. I want to know yours. I want to know how it became what it is today, why certain decisions were made, what you value about what you've built and what you want it to make possible from here.

Then we look at the business itself. Together, we can identify what is supporting your growth, what may be making it harder and where a different approach could make a meaningful difference.

The Starting Process

I don't start with answers. I start by understanding.

We begin with a conversation about your business and what you're hoping to accomplish. If we decide it makes sense to work together, the first paid step is a Diagnostic Assessment.

I'll review the information you share, evaluate how the business is operating and develop recommendations. We'll then meet to discuss what I've found and what I believe deserves your attention.

You can take those recommendations and move forward independently, or we can discuss additional work together. Any further engagement is scoped separately.
