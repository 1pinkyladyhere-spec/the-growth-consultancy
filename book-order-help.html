Book Order Help

Need Help With Your Book Order?

Ready for Success and its companion workbook are printed and fulfilled by Lulu. If you experience a problem with your order, contacting Lulu directly is the fastest way to get help.

Before You Contact Lulu

Having your order information ready can help Lulu address your issue more quickly.

Please have available:

  • Your Lulu order number
  • The email address used to place your order
  • Your order confirmation and shipping information
  • Your packing slip, when applicable
  • A clear description of what happened
  • Clear photographs if your book arrived damaged, defective or incorrect

If you received the wrong item, photograph both the item you received and the packing slip.

If Your Book Arrived Damaged or Has a Printing Defect

Take clear photographs showing the problem and keep the book and its packaging while your issue is being resolved.

Lulu handles qualifying damaged and defective product claims. These issues should be reported promptly; Lulu's current policy states that qualifying damaged, defective or incorrectly packaged orders must be reported within 30 days of the shipment date.

Contact Lulu Support →

If You Received the Wrong Item or Something Is Missing

Check your order information and packing slip first. Some orders may arrive in more than one shipment.

If you received an incorrect item, take a photograph of the item you received and your packing slip before contacting Lulu.

Contact Lulu Support →

Returns & Replacements

These books are printed on demand. Lulu does not generally accept returns of physical products simply because a customer changes their mind.

Damaged, defective or incorrectly packaged products are handled separately under Lulu's current replacement policy.

Because Lulu's policies may change, please refer to Lulu directly for the most current terms.
