When You Feel Overwhelmed by the Life Ahead of You

By Elle Lundy | Starting Point

When life changes unexpectedly, it can be difficult to know where to begin.

Perhaps you're navigating a divorce or another major life transition. The future you once envisioned looks different now, and you're facing decisions you never expected to have to make.

You may find yourself lying awake at night, wondering how you're going to manage everything.

I understand what it's like to face a future that looks nothing like the one you planned. I've also learned how overwhelming life can become when we place the responsibility of figuring out our entire future on ourselves today.

One of the things I often remind my coaching clients is this:

You don't need to try to heal everything today. We just need to take care of what needs to be taken care of today.

There will be decisions to make, emotions to work through and adjustments along the way. But they don't all require your attention at this very moment.

Give yourself permission to be where you are without expecting yourself to have all the answers.

There is still a beautiful life ahead of you, even if you can't see what it looks like yet.

Through Starting Point and my Healing Without Bitterness™ private coaching, I support individuals navigating divorce and other significant life changes.

You don't have to navigate this season alone.

If you would benefit from some additional support, I invite you to connect with me through Starting Point.
