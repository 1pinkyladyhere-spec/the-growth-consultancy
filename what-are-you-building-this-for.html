The question behind the book

Growth should create more choices, not simply more work.

What Are You Building This For? is for entrepreneurs who want to think differently about growth before adding another service, employee, income stream or responsibility.

Elle Lundy explores capacity, structure, founder dependence, boundaries and intentional growth while asking a larger question: What is your business supposed to make possible?

The goal is not to tell you what kind of life to want. It is to help you notice whether the business you are building supports the life you actually want to live.

This book may be for you if

You have built something successful, but the next stage feels heavier than it should.

Too much still depends on you.
You are the person everyone calls, asks or waits for.
You are considering another opportunity.
Before you add it, you want to know whether it belongs in the business and the life you are building.
You want growth to lead somewhere.
You want more than a bigger number. You want your business to create options for your time, money and attention.
