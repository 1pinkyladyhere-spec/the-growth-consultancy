What Are You Building This For?

Building something you believe in is hard.

There will be obstacles. There will be fear. There will be doubt. There will be moments when the goal feels much farther away than it did when you started.

That's why knowing what you're building isn't enough. You need to know why you're building it.

Because when the obstacles come—and they will—your why gives you something to come back to. It helps you make decisions, keep moving and recognize when what you're doing is actually taking you closer to the life and goals you're working toward.

Elle Lundy speaks about what it takes to build with intention, face the fear and doubt that come with growth, and keep making progress toward the reason you started in the first place.

What Are You Building This For?

Most people have an idea of what they want to build. Fewer have stopped to consider what it will require of them to get there—or whether what they're building will actually create the life they want.

In What Are You Building This For?, Elle challenges audiences to look beyond the goal and get clear about the reason behind it.

Because the path won't always go according to plan. Fear shows up. Doubt shows up. Circumstances change. And at some point, the reason you started may be the very thing that reminds you why it's worth continuing—or helps you recognize when it's time to build something different.

Through personal storytelling and practical perspective, Elle invites audiences to think differently about success, the obstacles between where they are and where they want to be, and what they're willing to do to move forward.

Audiences leave asking themselves one question:
What am I building this for?
About Elle

Strategy, storytelling and lived experience.

Elle Lundy is a business growth strategist, author and speaker who believes that knowing what you want is only the beginning. Building it requires clarity, structure and a willingness to keep moving when things don't go according to plan.

As the founder of The Growth Consultancy, Elle works with established entrepreneurs who have reached a point where the way they built their business may no longer support where they want to go next. Her work also includes helping business owners develop books strategically—not simply to become authors, but to strengthen their brand, establish authority and support the larger direction of their business.

Her perspective comes from more than business strategy. Elle has built businesses, changed direction, started over and learned firsthand that the definition of success can change as your life changes.

An actor with experience in film and commercial work, Elle also brings years of experience communicating in front of audiences and cameras. That background, combined with her work in business strategy and brand development, shapes the way she communicates: with clarity, presence and an understanding that how an idea is presented matters almost as much as the idea itself.

She is the author of Ready for Success and What Are You Building This For?, two books that explore different parts of the same question: Are you prepared not only to want something different, but to do what it takes to build it?

Whether she's working with a business owner or speaking to a room, Elle's work comes back to one thing: helping people get clear about where they're going, why it matters and what it will take to move forward.

Who This Talk Is For

Clear audiences. A direct message.

Established business owners

Who have built successful businesses but are questioning whether the way they are running them can support their next stage of growth—or the life they want outside of work.

Entrepreneurs preparing for growth

Who need to consider what that growth will require of their time, priorities, systems and responsibilities.

Women rebuilding after major life change

Women rebuilding after divorce, loss of income or another major life change who are figuring out how to create financial stability and decide what they want their next chapter to look like.

People pursuing a meaningful goal

Who find themselves stalled by fear, doubt, uncertainty, money or the opinions of other people.
