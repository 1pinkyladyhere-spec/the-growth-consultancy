A Place to Begin

How to Make Money After Divorce When You Don't Know Where to Start

There are so many things no one prepares you for when a marriage ends.

The grief. The decisions. The paperwork. The strange feeling of looking at a life you thought would look one way and realizing it's going to look completely different.

And then there is money.

Maybe you haven't had to support yourself on your income alone for years. Maybe you put your career on hold while you raised your family. Maybe you worked, but your income was never meant to carry the entire household.

And now it has to.

That realization can be terrifying.

You may find yourself lying awake trying to calculate what you need to earn. Looking at job postings and wondering whether you're qualified anymore. Thinking about starting something of your own but having absolutely no idea what that would be.

And underneath all of it may be a question you haven't said out loud:

Am I going to be okay?

I can't answer every part of that question for you.

But I can tell you this.

You do not have to have your entire life figured out right now.

You don't need a five-year plan. You don't need to suddenly know what your new career should be. And you don't have to make one enormous decision that fixes everything.

You need somewhere to start.

And your starting point may be closer than you think.

There may be skills you've stopped noticing because you've used them for years. Experience you've never considered valuable because no one handed you a paycheck for it. Ideas you've dismissed because they didn't look like a traditional career.

That doesn't mean every idea will become a business or that there's an easy answer waiting around the corner.

It means we don't count you out simply because your life changed.

That's why I created Starting Point.

It's a place to begin when you know something has to change but you don't know what comes next. You'll find free resources there to help you start thinking differently about what you already have, what may be possible, and where you might go from here.

You don't have to solve everything today.

Let's just find your starting point.

You don't have to know what comes next yet.

Starting Point is a place to begin exploring what may be possible from here.
